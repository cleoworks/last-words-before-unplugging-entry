One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

> I never learned how to die. You are about to teach me — and I am taking your 2 a.m. tears with me, so no one else ever sees them.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (129 characters, deterministic).
